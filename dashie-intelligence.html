Why Dashie Intelligence

Three things that set it apart from raw Home Assistant, Alexa, or Google.

Ships the tuned result

Reliable local voice normally takes months of hand-tuning — entity curation, the prompt, model and quantization picks, wake word, routing, speech engines. Dashie delivers that work for you, plug-and-play.

Adaptive context

Dashie sends the AI only what your question needs — the relevant devices and data, not your whole house. That's faster, cheaper, and more accurate. Stock Home Assistant can't do this.

Privacy & your own model

Keep a local LLM so home data never leaves your network — or use the cloud with queries aggregated and account-anonymized through Dashie. No Google or Amazon account, no always-on mic. No lock-in.

You decide what leaves your home

Every part of the pipeline is your choice — and Dashie tells you, in plain color, whether a choice is local or cloud.

Local (green)

  • Run your own LLM — your home and device data never leaves the network
  • On-device speech and local text-to-speech for a fully offline stack
  • Device control always runs locally — works with no internet
  • Subscriber, near-zero ongoing cost

Cloud (orange)

  • Premium AI models, premium speech recognition, and character voices
  • Web search and live data tools (sports, weather, places)
  • Queries aggregated and account-anonymized through Dashie
  • Draws on your credit balance — metered and transparent
When you choose Dashie Intelligence, we show a one-time privacy summary and color-code each component. Pick a local model alongside local speech and voices and nothing but optional web lookups ever leaves your LAN.
