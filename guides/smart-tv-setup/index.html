Setting Up Dashie on Your Smart TV

Last updated: May 2026

Dashie runs great on the big screen. If your TV is built on Android, you can install Dashie like any other app and turn that screen into a family dashboard whenever the TV isn't being used for shows or games.

Which TVs work

Dashie is an Android app, so any TV built on Android works. Here's the current picture:

Smart TV Platform Supported How to install
Google TV / Chromecast with Google TV Yes Google Play Store
Amazon Fire TV Yes Amazon Appstore
Android TV (built into many smart TVs) Yes Google Play Store or sideload
Apple TV No —
Roku No —
Samsung Tizen / LG webOS No —

Not sure what your TV runs on? The remote and on-screen UI are usually a dead giveaway: a Google Assistant button and Play Store icons point to Google / Android TV; an Alexa button and the Fire-orange home screen point to Fire TV. If you see Roku's purple grid or Apple's tvOS, Dashie isn't supported on that TV today — a Google TV or Fire TV stick plugged into HDMI is the easiest workaround.

Installing Dashie

Google TV / Android TV

Open the Google Play Store on your TV, search for Dashie, and install. Updates roll out automatically through the Play Store.

Amazon Fire TV

Open the Amazon Appstore on your Fire TV, search for Dashie, and install. Updates roll out automatically through the Amazon Appstore.

Sideloading (Android TVs without the right store)

Some Android TVs ship without the Google Play Store. In that case, install Dashie's APK directly. The full step-by-step is in the Android Sideloading guide — the same steps work on a TV with ADB enabled.

Signing in

On first launch, Dashie shows a sign-in screen with a QR code. You don't need to type anything on the TV — just scan the QR code with your phone (or visit the URL it shows and enter the short pairing code) and sign in with Google there. The TV will pair within a few seconds and start syncing your calendars, photos, family, and settings.

QR pairing flow

The pairing code expires after ~8 minutes. If it expires before you scan, cancel and Dashie will issue a new one. Once paired, you won't see this screen again on this TV — you stay signed in unless you explicitly sign out.

Tips for the big-screen experience

Remote-friendly navigation

Dashie's layout is designed for D-pad navigation. Use the arrow keys to move between widgets, the center button to select, and the Back button to step out. The OK / Enter button opens a focused widget in full-screen mode (great for the calendar or photos).

When to launch Dashie

A common pattern is to leave Dashie running and use the TV's input-switch button to flip back to your normal content. On Google TV and Fire TV you can also set Dashie to launch when the TV wakes from sleep, so the dashboard is the first thing your family sees in the morning.

Voice control

On TVs with a microphone remote (Google TV, Fire TV) you can use the platform's built-in voice search to "Open Dashie" without navigating through the launcher.
