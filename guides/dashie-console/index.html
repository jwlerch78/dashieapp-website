Using the Dashie Console

Last updated: June 2026 • Console v0.1.120

The Dashie Console is where you manage everything about your Dashie household — your tablets, family members, calendars, photo albums, AI personality, voice settings, and account. It's the same UI whether you reach it on the web or from inside Home Assistant.

Two ways to open the Console

Web Visit dashieapp.com/console in any browser. Works on every platform.
In HA Install the Dashie Console app and open it from your HA sidebar. Adds live device metrics, history charts, and HA-side controls.

Most of the Console works identically across both paths. The Devices section is where they meaningfully differ — the in-HA Console can talk directly to your tablets through Home Assistant and surface things the web Console can't see. Family, Photos, and AI/Voice are the same either way.

What you need

  • A Dashie account — sign up at dashieapp.com
  • For the in-HA path: the Dashie Console app installed in Home Assistant. See Set up Dashie in Home Assistant (Part 4) for the install.

Opening the Console

Sign-in is the one place the two paths look visibly different. Both end at the same Console UI.

Web dashieapp.com/console

Open dashieapp.com/console in a browser. Click Sign in with Google (or your account provider). You'll be sent to Google's standard sign-in and bounced back to the Console.

The session sticks in browser cookies — close the tab, open it later, you're still signed in.

In HA Dashie Console app

Click Dashie Console in your HA sidebar (or open Settings → Apps → Dashie Console → Open Web UI).

First time, you'll see an 8-character code. On your phone or laptop, open dashieapp.com/link, sign in, enter the code, and approve. The HA Console stays signed in across HA reboots.

Managing your devices

Devices is your fleet view — every Dashie tablet, TV, or kiosk display tied to your account. Each device shows up as a card; click into one for details. The in-HA Console adds a layer of live data the web Console doesn't have access to, because it can read your Home Assistant entities directly.

Web What you can do

  • See every device on your account
  • Rename devices, set room labels
  • Change theme, sleep schedule, screensaver mode
  • Set photo album, layout mode, font/display size
  • Archive old devices

Per-device settings sync to the device the next time it checks in with the Dashie cloud (usually within seconds while it's online).

In HA Everything from web, plus:

  • Live metrics — battery, RAM, Wi-Fi, current screenshot, refreshed every few seconds
  • History charts — click any metric chip to see 1h / 24h / week / custom-range graphs
  • Direct controls — turn the screen on/off, adjust brightness/volume, lock the device, toggle screensaver immediately
  • Discovered devices — tablets running Dashie on your network that aren't yet on your account

The in-HA Console reaches your tablets through the Dashie HA integration, so controls happen in real time over your local network.

Technical view toggle

In the in-HA Console, look for the Show technical details button at the top of the Devices list. It flips each card between a simple summary view ("what's playing on this dashboard") and a full technical view (every metric, screenshot, and control). On a fresh HA install it defaults to ON; the web Console defaults to OFF.

Family members and calendars

The Family section is where you add the people in your household, link their Google calendars, and decide whose schedule appears on each dashboard. This is identical on both paths — same UI, same data, backed by your Dashie account.

For each member you can: link a Google account (for calendar sync), pick which calendars are visible to Dashie, assign a color and icon, and decide which devices should display their events.

Deep dive

For the full walkthrough — adding members, linking Google, configuring calendar visibility per-device — see Family Management and Calendar Setup.

AI personality and voice

Under AI & Voice (sometimes shown as "Dashie Intelligence" on the sidebar) you'll find the settings that shape how Dashie talks back. Personality presets, custom prompts, wake word, voice selection, and which AI model handles requests. Same UI on both paths.

Voice changes propagate to every device on your account: pick a new personality on the Console, and the next time the tablet wakes up it'll use that personality. Wake-word changes take effect at the device's next reboot or settings refresh.

Deep dive

See Dashie Intelligence Setup for the full personality-and-voice walkthrough, plus Voice Control Setup for the wake-word details.

Photos

Photos lets you build albums that show up on your dashboards' photo widgets and on the screensaver. Add photos by uploading from your phone or computer, or by linking a Google Photos album. Same UI on both paths.

Each device picks its photo source independently — useful when one tablet shows family photos in the kitchen and another shows vacation pics in the bedroom. You choose the album per-device in the Devices section.

Troubleshooting

I'm signed in on web but the in-HA Console asks me to sign in again

That's expected. The web Console uses browser cookies; the in-HA Console runs inside a separate ingress-isolated session and signs in independently via the device-flow code at dashieapp.com/link. You only need to do it once per HA install.

My device shows up on web but not in the in-HA Devices list

The in-HA Console only sees devices that are also registered as HA devices via the Dashie integration. Make sure you've completed Parts 1–3 of the HA setup guide. If the device exists in HA but isn't on your Dashie account, the in-HA Console will surface it as "Discovered" with an Adopt button.

Changes I make on the Console don't appear on my tablet

Most settings sync within seconds while the device is online. If a setting isn't taking, check that the device is awake and connected (battery icon, last-seen timestamp on the device card). Photo album changes take effect at the next photo refresh interval (a few minutes by default).

Need more help?

If something isn't behaving as described, contact us with your account email, the device name, and what you were trying to do. Screenshots help.
