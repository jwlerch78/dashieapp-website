Deleting Your Dashie Account

Dashie lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to request deletion and exactly what is removed.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Dashie account directly within the app:

  1. Open the Dashie app and go to Settings.
  2. Select Account.
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data are permanently deleted right away.

If you no longer have the app installed, or prefer to request deletion by email, contact us at support@dashieapp.com from the email address associated with your Dashie account and we will process the deletion for you.

What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following are permanently and immediately removed from our systems:

  • Your account profile and login record
  • All calendars and connected calendar accounts
  • Google OAuth authentication tokens (immediately revoked)
  • Photos and photo albums you have added
  • Family members and family settings
  • Chores, rewards, and point balances
  • Voice profiles and wake-word recordings
  • AI assistant interaction history
  • Saved locations
  • All registered devices and their settings
  • All app preferences and dashboard settings

What Data Is Kept

After deletion, we retain one item only: a record of your email address noting that the account had previously used a free trial. We keep this solely to prevent the same person from repeatedly deleting and recreating an account to obtain additional free trials. No other personal data, content, calendar information, photos, or settings are retained.

Retention Period

Account deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no recovery or grace period — once your account is deleted, the data listed above cannot be restored.

The single retained item (the email address used for free-trial tracking, described above) is kept indefinitely for the purpose of trial-abuse prevention.
